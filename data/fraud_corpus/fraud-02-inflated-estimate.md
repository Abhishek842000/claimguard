# Inflated body-shop estimate

Fraudulent or loosely affiliated shops write estimates that replace an
entire bumper assembly, sensors, and a radiator support after a parking-lot
tap. The estimate is often 3–5× a reasonable repair range for the visible
damage. Photos that show cosmetic scuffs next to a five-figure estimate
should be scored as an inflation signal even when the collision itself is
real.

Cross-check claimed amount against photo severity. If the vision
assessment is minimal or low and the demanded payout is in the high
thousands without a teardown, require a desk review and an independent
appraisal before auto-resolve.
