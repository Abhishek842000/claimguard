# Phantom passenger

A third occupant appears on the claim form after the first notice of loss
listed only the driver. Medical specials are billed for soft-tissue injury
with no emergency-room record on the day of loss. There is no mention of
the passenger in the police narrative.

When passenger count changes between FNOL, adjuster notes, and the
estimate, or when injury claims appear without a corresponding vehicle
damage severity, treat the injury portion as high-risk even if vehicle
damage is legitimate.
