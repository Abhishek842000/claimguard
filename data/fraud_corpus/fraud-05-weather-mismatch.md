# Weather claim with no supporting storm

Comprehensive hail or wind claims are frequently filed after a widely
reported regional storm — and sometimes filed in cities the storm never
reached. If the loss location and date have no hail, thunderstorm-wind,
or tropical event in the NOAA storm-events record (or the frozen
ClaimGuard fixture derived from that shape), the weather narrative is
ungrounded.

A rooftop or hood with circular dings can still be prior-year hail.
Absence of a storm event on the date of loss is a medium-to-high fraud
signal and should be cited with the weather lookup result, not with
model speculation.
