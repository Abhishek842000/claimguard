# Home arson-for-profit indicators

Fires reported shortly after a large coverage increase, after a foreclosure
notice, or when the dwelling has been vacant, deserve an origin-and-cause
review. Multiple points of origin, the absence of contents that would
normally be present, and a delayed 911 call are classic indicators.

A Coverage A fire can still be accidental. The fraud signal is the
combination of financial pressure, vacancy, and an origin story that
conflicts with the fire department narrative — not the mere fact of fire.
