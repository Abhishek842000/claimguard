# Prior damage claimed as new

Rust inside a crease, faded paint in a dent, or a shop invoice for the
same panel from a prior year indicate old damage presented as this
accident. Adjuster notes that say "looks fresh" while photos show
oxidized metal should be treated as contradictory, not as confirmation.

Compare the current photos to any prior claim on the same VIN. Matching
prior-damage locations without a new impact zone is a high-severity
signal.
