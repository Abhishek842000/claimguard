# Theft recovery with inconsistent damage

A stolen-vehicle claim that is recovered within 48 hours with no hot-wire
evidence, keys "left in the car," or damage that looks like a parking
scrape rather than a theft recovery, is inconsistent with a total-loss
demand. Some insureds report theft after a collision they caused.

Require the police report number (Part D theft condition). If recovery
photos show the same panel damage as a prior unpaid collision estimate,
treat as possible recharacterization of a collision as theft.
