# Mechanical breakdown dressed as collision

Engine or transmission failure presented as "I hit something" with no
impact point, no third party, and photos of an intact bumper is an
attempt to convert an excluded mechanical breakdown into a collision
claim.

Part D excludes loss due and confined to wear, mechanical, or electrical
breakdown. If the narrative never describes an object struck and the
estimate is powertrain-only, coverage should be excluded and the file
scored as a presentation risk rather than a crash.
