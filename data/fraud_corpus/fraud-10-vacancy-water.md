# Water damage after extended vacancy

A sudden-pipe story is harder to sustain when utilities were shut off and
the dwelling was vacant more than 60 days. Mold and long-run staining
contradict a "yesterday" overflow. The HO-3 vacancy clause also removes
vandalism and some water coverage after 60 days empty.

If adjuster notes mention "looks like it sat" or neighbors report no
occupancy, retrieve the vacancy exclusion and treat the claim as
insufficient-information or excluded until occupancy dates are proven.
