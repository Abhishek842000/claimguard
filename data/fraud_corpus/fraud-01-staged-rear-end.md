# Staged rear-end / swoop-and-squat

A recurring auto-fraud pattern is a low-speed rear-end collision that the
claimant describes as a sudden stop by the lead vehicle. Indicators include:
the same lead vehicle appearing in multiple unrelated claims; a delay before
police are called; occupants who cannot describe the other driver's plate;
and repair estimates that list structural parts on a bumper-only impact.

When the FNOL narrative is a textbook "I was stopped at a light and got
hit from behind" but the photos show only scuffs, treat the claim as
elevated-risk until a police report and the third-party carrier confirm
the event. Entity-graph matches on VIN, phone, or body shop across prior
staged-accident rings are high-severity signals.
