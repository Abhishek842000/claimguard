# Late report plus third-party gaps

A collision reported more than 30 days after the alleged date, with no
police report and an unidentifiable third party, is a common opportunistic
pattern: the insured needs a repair, invents a date, and hopes for a
first-party collision payout.

The policy duties-after-loss clause requires prompt notice. Late notice
alone is not fraud, but late notice plus a missing third party plus
photos that do not match the stated impact geometry is a stacked signal.
