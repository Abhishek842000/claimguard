# Coverage A — Dwelling

## Insuring Agreement

We cover the dwelling on the residence premises shown in the Declarations,
including structures attached to the dwelling, and materials and supplies
located on or next to the residence premises used to construct, alter, or
repair the dwelling or other structures on the residence premises.

This is a special-form (open perils) coverage for the dwelling: we cover
risk of direct physical loss to property described in Coverage A unless
the loss is excluded in Section I — Exclusions.

## Limit and Deductible (Specimen)

- Coverage A — Dwelling: $350,000
- Section I deductible: $1,000 per occurrence unless a different deductible
  is shown for a specific peril (for example, a $2,500 wind/hail deductible
  in coastal or hail-prone territories)

We pay only the amount of a covered loss that exceeds the deductible.

## Replacement Cost — Dwelling

We will pay the cost to repair or replace the damaged dwelling, without
deduction for depreciation, provided:

1. The dwelling is insured for at least 80 percent of its replacement cost
   at the time of loss; and
2. You repair or replace the damaged property.

If the dwelling is not repaired or replaced, we will pay actual cash value,
not to exceed the Coverage A limit.

## Additional Coverages

Debris removal, reasonable repairs to protect the property from further
damage, and trees, shrubs, and plants (named perils only, $500 per item)
are included, subject to the limits in this form.
