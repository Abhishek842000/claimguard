# Section I — Exclusions

We do not insure for loss caused directly or indirectly by any of the
following. Such loss is excluded regardless of any other cause or event
contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss.

## Water / Flood

Water. This means:

1. Flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water,
   or spray from any of these, all whether or not driven by wind;
2. Water or water-borne material that backs up through sewers or drains
   or that overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump, or related
   equipment; and
3. Water or water-borne material below the surface of the ground,
   including water that exerts pressure on or seeps or leaks through a
   building, sidewalk, driveway, foundation, swimming pool, or other
   structure.

This exclusion does not apply to sudden and accidental discharge or
overflow of water or steam from a plumbing, heating, or household
appliance on the residence premises. A slow leak over weeks, or water
that entered because of flood or storm surge, is not sudden and accidental
discharge.

## Earth Movement

Earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, mudflow,
and earth sinking, rising, or shifting.

## Neglect

Neglect by an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve
property at and after the time of a loss.

## Intentional Loss

Intentional loss, meaning any loss arising out of any act an insured
commits or conspires to commit with the intent to cause a loss. This
exclusion applies even if the insured is insane or intoxicated.

## Vacancy and Unoccupancy

We do not cover vandalism, malicious mischief, glass breakage, or water
damage if the dwelling has been vacant for more than 60 consecutive days
immediately before the loss. A dwelling being constructed is not vacant.

## Wear, Deterioration, and Faulty Work

Wear and tear, marring, deterioration; inherent vice, latent defect;
smog, rust, mold; settling, cracking of pavements or walls; birds,
vermin, rodents; and faulty, inadequate, or defective planning, zoning,
design, workmanship, or materials. If a covered peril results, we cover
only the resulting sudden accidental damage, not the cost to correct the
defect.

## Ordinance or Law

The enforcement of any ordinance or law requiring or regulating the
construction, demolition, remodeling, or repair of property, except as
provided under the Ordinance or Law additional coverage.
