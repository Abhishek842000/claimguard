# Coverage C — Personal Property

## Insuring Agreement

We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is
anywhere in the world. At your request, we also cover personal property
owned by others while the property is on the part of the residence
premises occupied by an insured.

Coverage C is named-perils coverage. We cover direct physical loss to
covered property caused by a peril listed below, unless the loss is
excluded.

## Named Perils (abridged)

Fire or lightning; windstorm or hail; explosion; riot; aircraft; vehicles;
smoke; vandalism or malicious mischief; theft; falling objects; weight of
ice, snow, or sleet; accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam
from a plumbing, heating, or air-conditioning system (sudden and
accidental); sudden and accidental tearing apart of a heating system;
freezing of a plumbing system (if you used reasonable care to maintain
heat); sudden and accidental damage from an artificially generated
electrical current.

## Theft

Theft includes attempted theft and loss of property from a known place
when it is likely that the property has been stolen. A theft claim
requires a police report. Theft of jewelry, watches, and furs is limited
to $1,500 unless a Scheduled Personal Property endorsement is shown.

## Limit (Specimen)

Coverage C — Personal Property: $175,000 (50 percent of Coverage A unless
otherwise scheduled). Special limits apply to money ($200), securities
($1,500), jewelry ($1,500), silverware ($2,500), and firearms ($2,500).
