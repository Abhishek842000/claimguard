# Part D — Coverage For Damage To Your Auto

## Insuring Agreement — Collision

We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto caused by
collision, minus the applicable deductible shown in the Declarations.
Collision means the upset of your covered auto or a non-owned auto, or its
impact with another vehicle or object.

Loss caused by the following is treated as collision when the insured
elects collision coverage: impact with another vehicle, impact with a
fixed object such as a curb, guardrail, or parked car, and rollover.

## Who Is An Insured — Collision

You, a family member, and any other person using the covered auto with
your express or implied permission are insureds for this coverage, subject
to the exclusions in this Part D.

## Limit of Liability — Collision

Our limit of liability for loss is the lesser of:

1. The actual cash value of the stolen or damaged property at the time of
   loss, reduced by the applicable deductible; or
2. The amount necessary to repair or replace the property with other
   property of like kind and quality; or
3. The limit shown in the Declarations for Collision, if any.

An adjustment for depreciation and physical condition will be made in
determining actual cash value. If a repair or replacement results in better
than like kind and quality, we will not pay for the amount of the betterment.

## Payment of Loss

We may pay for loss in money or repair or replace the damaged or stolen
property. We may, at our expense, return any stolen property to you. If we
return stolen property we will pay for any damage resulting from the theft.
We may keep all or part of the property at an agreed or appraised value.

## No Benefit to Bailee

This insurance shall not directly or indirectly benefit any carrier or
other bailee for hire.
