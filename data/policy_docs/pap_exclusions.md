# Part D — Exclusions

We will not pay for:

## Wear, Mechanical Breakdown, and Freezing

Loss due and confined to wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical
breakdown or failure, or road damage to tires. This exclusion does not
apply if the damage results from the total theft of your covered auto.

A claim that describes only an engine light, transmission slip, or failed
alternator, with no accidental impact, is a mechanical breakdown and is
not a covered collision or comprehensive loss.

## Racing and Competitive Driving

Loss to any auto while it is being used in any racing, speed, demolition,
or stunting activity, or while practicing or being prepared for such
activity. This includes any event at a track or course, whether or not
the event is sanctioned.

## Intentional Damage

Loss caused by an intentional act of an insured, or at the direction of
an insured. This exclusion applies even if the insured is insane or
intoxicated.

## Electronic Equipment

Loss to electronic equipment that receives or transmits audio, visual, or
data signals and that is not permanently installed, and to tapes, records,
discs, or other media used with that equipment.

## Public or Livery Conveyance

Loss to any auto while it is being used as a public or livery conveyance.
This exclusion does not apply to a share-the-expense car pool.

## War and Nuclear

Loss caused by war (declared or undeclared), civil war, insurrection,
rebellion, revolution, or nuclear reaction, radiation, or radioactive
contamination.

## Custom Furnishings

Loss to custom furnishings or equipment in or upon a pickup or van,
including special carpeting, insulation, furniture, bars, and cooking
equipment, unless the Customizing Equipment endorsement is shown.
