# Part D — Other Than Collision (Comprehensive)

## Insuring Agreement — Other Than Collision

We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto caused by
other than collision, minus the applicable deductible shown in the
Declarations. Other than collision includes, but is not limited to, the
following causes of loss:

- Missiles or falling objects
- Fire
- Theft or larceny
- Explosion or earthquake
- Windstorm, hail, water, or flood
- Malicious mischief or vandalism
- Riot or civil commotion
- Contact with a bird or animal
- Breakage of glass (if not caused by collision)

Loss caused by contact with a bird or animal, or breakage of glass, is
paid under Other Than Collision unless the insured elects to have it
treated as collision.

## Theft

If your covered auto is stolen, we will pay transportation expenses
incurred because of the theft. We will pay up to $30 per day, to a maximum
of $900, beginning 48 hours after the theft is reported to us and the
police, and ending when the auto is returned or we pay for its loss.

A theft claim requires a police report number. We may deny or delay
payment until the insured has reported the theft to law enforcement.

## Glass

We will pay for breakage of glass under Other Than Collision without
applying the deductible if the insured has purchased the windshield-full
coverage endorsement. Otherwise the Other Than Collision deductible applies.
