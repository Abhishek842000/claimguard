# Declarations — Personal Auto Policy PAP-CG-01

This specimen declarations page is used by ClaimGuard to ground deductible
and limit questions. Values shown here are the default book of business used
in the synthetic claim set unless a claim record overrides them.

## Named Insured and Vehicle

The policy insures the person shown as Named Insured and the covered auto
described by year, make, model, and VIN on the declarations. A temporary
substitute auto is a covered auto only while the described auto is out of
normal use because of breakdown, repair, servicing, loss, or destruction.

## Coverages and Limits (Specimen)

- Liability — Bodily Injury: $100,000 each person / $300,000 each accident
- Liability — Property Damage: $50,000 each accident
- Collision deductible: $500
- Other Than Collision (Comprehensive) deductible: $250
- Collision and Other Than Collision limit: actual cash value of the covered
  auto, not to exceed $50,000
- Uninsured Motorists Bodily Injury: $50,000 each person / $100,000 each accident
- Medical Payments: $5,000 each person
- Rental reimbursement: $30 per day, $900 maximum, only if shown as purchased

## Deductible Application

We pay only the amount of a covered loss that exceeds the deductible shown
for that coverage. Collision and Other Than Collision deductibles apply
separately. If a single accident involves both a collision loss and an
other-than-collision loss, only the higher deductible applies.

## Policy Period and Territory

Coverage applies to accidents and losses that occur during the policy period
shown and within the United States of America, its territories or
possessions, Puerto Rico, or Canada.
